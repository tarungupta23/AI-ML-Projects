Trained on real Netflix Prize ratings — 1,500 of the most active viewers, 1,350 titles, 765K real ratings. The engine underneath is a truncated SVD built by hand: power iteration with deflation on the mean-centered rating matrix, no numpy.linalg.svd or scipy.sparse.linalg.svds involved. Only movies a viewer never rated get a predicted score — every real rating they gave is left untouched.
